# Third-party notices for WordQuest AI 1.0.27.1 human teacher 3D prototypes

The adult **Emma**, **Sophia** and **Ethan** 3D review models are from the [Avatar Studio](https://github.com/memoriesrohit/avatar-studio) conversion of the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, pinned to commit `2fd9ec82fbe1613d40ae38aadcca3f410725f740`. The upstream input files are respectively `models/olivia_smart.glb`, `models/sophia_formal.glb`, and `models/ethan_casual.glb`. This candidate preview intentionally excludes the earlier VRoid/anime sample assets.
